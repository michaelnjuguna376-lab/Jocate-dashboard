Turning agricultural waste into value for East Africa

Clean, traceable agro-waste feedstock for partner companies

Jocate aggregates post-harvest residues from smallholder farmers and agro-processors — bagasse, rice husk, maize cobs and more — and supplies them to energy, construction and feed manufacturers across East Africa. All feedstock is quality-graded to ISO standards and traceable from collection hub to end-use facility.
